About GlenCrafts Lenasia

GlenCrafts Lenasia is a family run arts and crafts studio based in the heart of Lenasia. We specialise in designer and personalised gifting, from tissue boxes and bins to key holders and so much more, alongside custom parcels, hampers and décor for weddings, Eid, births and everyday celebrations.

Everything is designed with care, handcrafted from raw materials and finished to a standard we're proud to put our name on. If you can dream it, chances are we can craft it for you.

Our passion is creating beautiful, meaningful pieces that bring joy to your special moments. Whether it's a nikah parcel, a baby hamper, or a custom décor piece, we pour our heart into every creation.
